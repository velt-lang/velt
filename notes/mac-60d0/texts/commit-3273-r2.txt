std: an untaken offer is taken back; a dropped sleep's last request frees it in one step (#3207)

- A worker whose own offer is still there at an I/O tick wakes a sleeping worker for it again,
  or takes it back when none sleeps (sched_spread.velt reclaim). Only a worker out of work takes
  an offer, so an offer whose helper found work first stayed while every worker was busy, its
  tasks never polled (a stall, also with no sleep handed on).
- S_ASKS holds a mark that a drop request was served: a drop request ends itself and sets the
  mark in one atomic add, and whichever request leaves the word at the mark alone frees the
  sleep. Reading the dropped state before the decrement left a sleep unfreed when its drop was
  served on another worker than the request before it.
- The words other workers read (DUE, FIRED, S_HOME, S_OWNER) are stored and loaded with relaxed
  atomics (sched_mem.velt storeRelaxed), and insert's doc comment gives the reason DUE and FIRED
  need no release: one thread's stores to one word stay in order on both targets.
- Tests: the offers stress without any hand-over, and a handed sleep dropped after its request.
