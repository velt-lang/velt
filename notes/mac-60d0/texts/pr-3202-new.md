Part of #3097 (step 1 and gather-back). Closes #3117.

A call between two workers of the executor in Velt cost a full kernel wake on each hop: the woken worker had just parked in `epoll_wait`/`kevent`. Once step 2 lets tasks spread, a call often crosses workers, so that cost becomes the common case.

- **Spin before parking** (sched_spin.velt). A worker with nothing to run spins briefly on its inbox before it sleeps in its reactor, before its timer look. While it spins, its park word says so (`PARK_SPIN`, not asleep), so a waker on another worker pushes and takes the word down, with no system call, and the worker takes the task without a kernel wake or a context switch. Any wake ends the spin, one that brings no task too (the last task's end, an exit), and the worker's loop looks again as after a park.
  - The budget is adaptive: it starts at 20 µs, doubles up to 50 µs after a spin that found work, and halves down to off after one that didn't. A park that another worker ends soon after it began turns it back on at 2 µs (the worker's own timers and sockets don't), so an idle worker spins a few tens of microseconds once and then sleeps as before.
  - It is used only with several workers, and only while another worker is awake and not spinning itself. A pause (`atomicSpinHint`) between looks leaves the core's other hardware thread its turn.
- **Gather-back** (sched_gather.velt). Tasks that a burst spread over the workers come back to worker 0 once the load is gone. It concerns anchored tasks (ones that made a sleep): a task that may move runs in its waker's slot when another worker wakes it, but an anchored one goes to its home's inbox and pays a kernel wake for every call. An idle worker (tasks spread, and its last 16 takes of its inbox each found nothing else queued on it) hands the tasks woken onto it back to worker 0, unless worker 0 is loaded, lazy or stalled. What counts is the queue, not whether the worker had parked: a caller's next call may land before the worker reaches its park. An anchored task goes along only when it can carry its sleeps. A busy worker takes its inbox with tasks queued, which ends the run, so a burst's spread stays in place: this is the hysteresis against offers.
- **#3117.** A spare worker that is asked to retire while a task the rescue moved to it sleeps there now carries the sleep with the task to worker 0 and retires. Before, the spare declined to retire while the sleep was pending (sched_relax.velt `keeps`), so worker 0 stayed shared and never became lazy again.
- **Inlining.** `schedShare`'s forwarding is a loop instead of recursion, and it is `@inline`, as `schedBox` now is. This keeps both inlined into hot loops when a program is split into two codegen units (with #3173). `steal` and `borrow` are `@inline` into the idle loop, as LLVM had them before the offers carried sleeps, and so is sched_mem.velt `copy`, which copies every async call's initial state (a few moves at a constant size).
